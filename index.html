High School Developer • Tech Enthusiast

Hey everyone, I am Riddhiman, a high school coding and tech enthusiast who loves to build capstone projects that solve practical, everyday problems through technology. I focus on areas where off-the-shelf software falls short—like international students balancing fluctuating rosters under strict immigration labor caps.

I also founded Oak Technologies, a collaborative coding group where my friends and I turn creative ideas into functioning technical reality. Previously, I placed in the top 20% globally in the final round of the International Computer Science Competition 2025 and earned a Silver honor at the LSP 2025 nationals.

My technical work emphasizes end-to-end implementation: from designing strongly-typed schemas and handling structured JSON responses to compiling client packages and writing deterministic mathematical calculus.
